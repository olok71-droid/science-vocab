2. Triangle Trade & Middle Passage

Vocabulary Summary 기반

Triangle Trade route (3 stages)

BBC KS3 History

The trading system linked three continents — Europe, Africa, the Americas.

이 무역 체계는 세 대륙(유럽·아프리카·미주)을 이었다.

Stage 1 (Europe → Africa): Ships sailed from Europe carrying manufactured goods (cloth, guns, alcohol).

1단계 (유럽 → 아프리카): 유럽 배가 공산품(천·총·술)을 싣고 출발.

Stage 2 (Africa → Americas): The Middle Passage — enslaved Africans were transported across the Atlantic in horrific conditions.

2단계 (아프리카 → 미주): Middle Passage — 노예가 된 아프리카인이 끔찍한 환경 속에 대서양을 건넘.

Stage 3 (Americas → Europe): Ships returned with goods from plantations — sugar, cotton, tobacco, coffee.

3단계 (미주 → 유럽): 배는 플랜테이션 생산물(설탕·면화·담배·커피)을 싣고 귀환.

Key vocabulary (Vocabulary Summary)

• Enslaved Person — person with no freedom or rights.

• 노예가 된 사람 — 자유와 권리가 없는 사람.

• Resistance — ways enslaved people fought back (rebellion, escape).

• 저항 — 노예가 맞선 방법 (반란, 탈출).

• Abolition / Abolitionists — ending slavery / people who campaigned for this.

• 폐지 / 폐지론자 — 노예제를 끝냄 / 이를 위해 운동한 사람들.

• Human Rights — freedoms and rights every person deserves.

• 인권 — 모든 사람이 받아야 할 자유와 권리.

Useful adjectives for stronger writing (Vocab Summary 8번)

For describing slavery and its impact: widespread, significant, gradual, rapid, effective, limited, transformative, brutal, inhumane, cruel, exploitative, resistant, courageous.

노예제와 그 영향을 묘사할 때 쓸 형용사: widespread(광범위한), significant(중요한), brutal(잔인한), inhumane(비인간적인), cruel(잔혹한), exploitative(착취적인), courageous(용감한) 등.

3. Modern Slavery — 5 Types & Real Cases

Lesson 10 - Types of Slavery PDF
📄 PDF

Bonded Labour / Debt Bondage

'Somebody is demanded to work in order to repay a loan; rather than pay it with money, they pay it with labour. Many slaves never end up paying their loans back, leaving them stuck in slavery for the rest of their lives.'

'대출을 갚기 위해 일하도록 강요당함. 돈 대신 노동으로 갚는다. 많은 노예가 대출을 다 갚지 못해 평생 노예 상태로 남는다.'

'If a slave dies before they have repaid the loan, the debt can be passed on to their partner or children. This then becomes descent-based slavery.'

'갚기 전에 죽으면 빚이 배우자나 자녀에게 넘어간다. 이것이 descent-based 노예제가 된다.'

Forced Labour

'Forced labour is any labour that people are forced to do against their own will. Any kind of forced labour is slavery. There are now estimated to be around 25 million people in forced labour.'

'강제 노동은 의지에 반하는 모든 노동. 어떤 형태의 강제 노동도 노예제. 약 2500만 명이 강제 노동 중으로 추정.'

'Migrants often have their passports taken from them by their workers, forcing them to stay in bondage.'

'이주자는 종종 고용주에게 여권을 빼앗겨 묶여 있게 된다.'

Child Slavery

'Worldwide, around one in four slaves are children. Children are used for physical labour, domestic slavery, fighting in armed conflict (e.g. the resistance army in Uganda) and commercial sexual abuse.'

'전 세계적으로 노예의 약 1/4이 아동. 노동·가사 노예·무력 분쟁(예: 우간다 저항군) 전투·성 착취에 동원됨.'

Live-in Migrant & Domestic Workers

'Slaves which are confined to private homes. They maintain the household by cleaning, cooking and sometimes helping with childcare. They are isolated from the slavery protections that are offered in a regular workplace.'

'가정집에 갇힌 노예. 청소·요리·육아 보조. 일반 직장에서 받는 노예 보호로부터 고립됨.'

'Employers can use the workers' migrant status to pressure them into work, by threatening to hand them into the authorities.'

'고용주는 이주민 신분을 이용해 당국에 신고하겠다고 협박한다.'

Marital Slavery (Forced Marriage)

What is Modern Slavery?

'Child marriage (under the age of 18 years old) is widely accepted in some cultures. However, this becomes slavery if:'

'18세 미만 결혼은 일부 문화에서 받아들여지나, 다음과 같으면 노예제:'

1. Neither person has agreed to the marriage (forced marriage).

1. 둘 다 동의하지 않은 결혼(강제 결혼).

2. Either person is being controlled or 'owned' by the other.

2. 한 사람이 다른 사람을 통제하거나 '소유'함.

3. Either person cannot leave the marriage, if they should want to.

3. 떠나려 해도 떠날 수 없는 경우.

Case Study: Adina (16, Uganda)

'After her parents died, Adina found work helping on a market stall in Kampala, Uganda. The woman who ran the stall told her to go with two men… Adina was put on a plane to the UK, collected by a man at the airport, taken to a house and locked in a kitchen. For the next two years, Adina was forced to live in this one room. She was also physically abused.'

'부모 사후 우간다 캄팔라 시장에서 일하던 16세 Adina. 가게 주인이 두 남자와 가라 함 → 영국행 비행기 → 한 집의 부엌에 갇힘 → 2년간 한 방에 갇혀 청소·요리 강요, 폭행도 당함.'

She escaped when a man fell asleep drunk and forgot to lock the door, hid in a ditch, flagged down a car, was dropped at Marylebone Police Station, London.

한 남자가 술 취해 문을 잠그지 않은 사이 도망, 도랑에 숨어 차를 세워 런던 Marylebone 경찰서에 도착.

Case Study: Theo (20, Greek Roma)

One of 54 Greek Roma gipsies (including 10 women) rescued from a flower farm in Penzance, Cornwall (UK). Local 'fixers' had promised them expenses, food, 'proper flats' and £34 a day. Instead they were housed in a barn with no heating, shared cans of dog food. The 'gangmaster' refused to pay, claiming they were in his debt for travel. When they tried to escape, heavies beat them up with sticks and threatened at gunpoint.

영국 콘월의 꽃 농장에서 구출된 그리스 로마니 집시 54명 중 한 명. 일당 £34 약속, 실제로는 난방 없는 헛간, 개 사료 통조림. 갱마스터는 빚이라며 임금 거부, 탈출 시도 시 막대기로 폭행, 총으로 위협.

Case Study: Danielle (15, Lithuania) & Jin Lai (12, China)

BBC KS3 History

Danielle was trafficked from Lithuania at 15. Friend offered a job in a London bar — abducted at airport by an Albanian. Sold, repeatedly raped and beaten, forced into prostitution. Escaped by saying they were going to a shop → contacted police → foster family → flown home.

Danielle는 15세에 리투아니아에서 인신매매. 런던 바 일자리 제안 → 공항에서 알바니아인에게 납치. 매매·반복적 성폭행·강제 매춘. 가게 간다고 속이고 탈출 → 경찰 → 위탁가정 → 귀국.

Jin Lai (12) from Fuijan, China. Families give boys to 'snakehead' gangs (traffickers, £20,000). The child must work to pay off debt. Threats to family for unpaid debts. Jin Lai worked 7 days/week in a restaurant, slept in a makeshift dormitory.

Jin Lai (12세, 중국 푸젠성). 가족이 '스네이크헤드' 갱(£20,000)에게 아이를 맡김. 아이는 빚 갚으려 일해야 함. 빚 못 갚으면 가족 협박. 식당에서 주 7일 일, 임시 기숙사에서 잠.

Stage 1 (Europe → Africa): Ships from Europe carried manufactured goods (cloth, guns, alcohol) to West Africa, exchanged for enslaved people.

1단계 (유럽 → 아프리카): 유럽 배가 공산품(천·총·술)을 서아프리카로 운반하고 노예와 교환.

Stage 2 — the Middle Passage (Africa → Americas): Enslaved Africans were transported across the Atlantic in horrific conditions on overcrowded ships.

2단계 — Middle Passage (아프리카 → 미주): 노예가 끔찍한 환경의 과적된 배로 대서양 횡단.

Stage 3 (Americas → Europe): Ships returned with plantation goods — sugar, cotton, tobacco, coffee — to be sold in Europe.

3단계 (미주 → 유럽): 배는 플랜테이션 생산물(설탕·면화·담배·커피)을 유럽으로 운반.

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Stage 1: Europe → Africa (manufactured goods)
  • Stage 2: Africa → Americas (enslaved people / Middle Passage)
  • Stage 3: Americas → Europe (sugar / cotton / tobacco / plantation goods)
💡 유럽→아프리카(공산품), 아프리카→미주(노예/Middle Passage), 미주→유럽(설탕·면화·담배).
Question 2 of 6 5 marks
Describe the FIVE TYPES of modern slavery (from Lesson 10 PDF).

✅ Model answer

1. Bonded Labour / Debt Bondage — working to repay a loan, often for life.

1. 채무 노예 — 대출을 갚기 위해 일함, 종종 평생.

2. Forced Labour — any labour against one's own will (~25 million people worldwide).

2. 강제 노동 — 의지에 반하는 모든 노동 (전 세계 약 2500만 명).

3. Child Slavery — about 1 in 4 slaves are children, used for labour, domestic work, armed conflict, sexual abuse.

3. 아동 노예 — 노예의 약 1/4이 아동, 노동·가사·전투·성착취에 동원.

4. Live-in Migrant / Domestic Workers — confined to private homes, isolated from workplace protections.

4. 가사 노동자 — 가정집에 갇혀 직장 보호로부터 고립.

5. Marital Slavery — forced marriage where one person controls or 'owns' the other.

5. 혼인 노예 — 한 사람이 다른 사람을 통제·소유하는 강제 결혼.

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Bonded labour / debt bondage
  • Forced labour
  • Child slavery
  • Domestic / live-in migrant workers
  • Marital slavery / forced marriage
💡 5종류 각 1점: bonded, forced, child, domestic, marital.
Question 3 of 6 4 marks
Write a PEEL paragraph using the sentence starters from the Vocabulary Summary to answer: 'The Middle Passage was the most brutal stage of the Triangle Trade.' Do you agree?

✅ Model answer

Point: The main reason the Middle Passage was the most brutal stage was the inhumane treatment of enslaved Africans during the journey.

Point: Middle Passage가 가장 잔혹한 단계였던 주된 이유는 항해 중 노예가 된 아프리카인에 대한 비인간적 처우였습니다.

Evidence: For example, eyewitness accounts (primary sources) show that enslaved people were chained together below deck for weeks, with very little food, water or sanitation.

Evidence: 예를 들어 목격자 진술(1차 사료)은 노예가 갑판 아래 몇 주 동안 사슬에 묶여 있었고, 음식·물·위생이 거의 없었음을 보여줍니다.

Explain: This means that many enslaved people did not survive the journey and the conditions were designed purely to maximise profit, treating humans as cargo.

Explain: 이는 많은 노예가 항해를 살아남지 못했고, 환경이 오직 이윤 극대화를 위해 설계되어 인간을 화물로 취급했음을 의미합니다.

Link: This matters because the Middle Passage shows the most extreme example of how the slave trade dehumanised people, which is why it is remembered as the most brutal stage today.

Link: 이것이 중요한 이유는 Middle Passage가 노예 무역이 인간을 비인간화한 가장 극단적 예시이기 때문입니다. 그래서 오늘날 가장 잔혹한 단계로 기억됩니다.

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Clear POINT using 'The main reason for X was…'
  • EVIDENCE using 'For example, the source shows…'
  • EXPLAIN using 'This means that…'
  • LINK using 'This matters because…'

For the next two years, Adina was forced to live in this one room.' What TYPE of modern slavery is this, and what TWO pieces of evidence in the extract support your answer?

✅ Model answer

This is Live-in Migrant / Domestic Slavery (also acceptable: Forced Labour, Child / young person slavery as she was 16).

이것은 가사 노동자(Live-in Migrant/Domestic) 노예제입니다 (혹은 Forced Labour, 16세니까 Child Slavery로도 인정).

Evidence 1: She was 'locked in a kitchen' — she was confined to a private home and isolated from protection.

근거 1: '부엌에 갇혔다' — 가정집에 갇혀 보호로부터 고립.

근거 2: '2년간 한 방에 강제로 살았다' — 떠날 자유가 없음, 노예제의 핵심 특징.

Question 5 of 6 4 marks
Explain how the Triangle Trade had BOTH short-term AND long-term impacts. Use the cause-effect words and conclusion phrases from the Vocabulary Summary.

✅ Model answer

In the short-term, the Triangle Trade led to enormous suffering for the millions of enslaved Africans who endured the brutal Middle Passage and were forced to work on plantations.

단기적으로, 삼각 무역은 잔혹한 Middle Passage를 견디고 플랜테이션에서 강제 노동한 수백만 명의 노예가 된 아프리카인에게 엄청난 고통을 주었습니다.

As a result, European countries (especially Britain) became extremely wealthy from sugar, cotton and tobacco profits, which contributed to the Industrial Revolution.

결과적으로 유럽 국가들(특히 영국)이 설탕·면화·담배 이윤으로 매우 부유해졌고, 이것이 산업혁명에 기여했습니다.

In the long-term, the trade caused widespread racial inequality and trauma that still affects communities today. African societies also suffered long-term population loss and economic damage.

장기적으로, 이 무역은 광범위한 인종 불평등과 트라우마를 일으켜 오늘날 공동체에도 영향을 미칩니다. 아프리카 사회도 장기적 인구 감소와 경제 피해를 입었습니다.

Overall, the consequences of the Triangle Trade were both immediate (suffering, profit) and long-lasting (inequality, economic impact).

전체적으로, 삼각 무역의 결과는 즉각적(고통·이윤)이면서 동시에 장기적(불평등·경제 영향)이었습니다.

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Identifies clear short-term impact (suffering / profit)
  • Identifies clear long-term impact (inequality / economic damage / trauma)
  • Uses cause-effect words (led to / contributed to / as a result)
  • Uses concluding phrase (overall / in conclusion)
💡 단기+장기 영향 + 인과 어휘 + 결론 어휘.
