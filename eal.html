1. Camp Green Lake (PART ONE)

Holes — opening of the novel
📄 PDF

The setting (배경)

'There is no lake at Camp Green Lake. There once was a very large lake here, the largest lake in Texas. That was over a hundred years ago. Now it is just a dry, flat wasteland.' (Holes, Ch 1)

'Camp Green Lake에는 호수가 없다. 옛날에는 텍사스에서 가장 큰 호수였지만 지금은 그저 마르고 평평한 황무지일 뿐이다.'

Summer temperature is around ninety-five degrees in the shade — if you can find any shade. The only trees are two old oaks on the eastern edge of the lake. The Warden owns the shade.

여름 기온은 그늘에서도 약 95도. 그늘은 동쪽 끝의 떡갈나무 두 그루뿐이고, 그 그늘은 Warden(여자 소장)의 것이다.

The yellow-spotted lizards (노란점 도마뱀)

'But you don't want to be bitten by a yellow-spotted lizard. That's the worst thing that can happen to you. You will die a slow and painful death. Always.' (Holes, Ch 1)

'노란점 도마뱀에게 물리지 마라. 가장 나쁜 일이다. 천천히 그리고 고통스럽게 죽는다. 언제나.'

Rattlesnakes and scorpions are dangerous too, but you usually survive. Yellow-spotted lizards = always death.

방울뱀과 전갈도 위험하지만 보통 살아남는다. 노란점 도마뱀 = 무조건 죽음.

Why bad boys come here (왜 캠프에 오나)

'Camp Green Lake is a camp for bad boys. If you take a bad boy and make him dig a hole every day in the hot sun, it will turn him into a good boy.' (Holes, Ch 2)

'Camp Green Lake은 나쁜 소년들을 위한 캠프이다. 나쁜 소년에게 매일 뜨거운 태양 아래에서 구덩이를 파게 하면 좋은 소년이 된다고 생각하는 사람도 있다.'

The judge gave Stanley a choice: jail or Camp Green Lake. Stanley had never been to camp before — he was from a poor family.

판사는 Stanley에게 선택을 줬다: 감옥이나 Camp Green Lake. Stanley는 가난해서 캠프에 가본 적이 없었다.

The Yelnats family curse (가족의 저주)

'It was all because of his no-good-dirty-rotten-pig-stealing-great-great-grandfather!' (Holes, Ch 3)

'다 그의 나쁘고 더럽고 썩어빠진 돼지를 훔친 고조할아버지 때문이다!'

The great-great-grandfather stole a pig from a one-legged Gypsy, who put a curse on him and all his descendants. Stanley's family don't really believe in curses, but they blame the curse whenever things go wrong.

고조할아버지가 외다리 집시에게서 돼지를 훔쳐서 그녀가 그와 후손들에게 저주를 걸었다. 가족은 저주를 진짜 믿지는 않지만, 일이 잘못될 때마다 저주 탓을 한다.

Stanley Yelnats name palindrome (이름 회문)

'Stanley Yelnats was spelled the same frontward and backward.' (Holes, Ch 4)

'Stanley Yelnats는 앞으로 읽어도 뒤로 읽어도 똑같이 쓰인다.' (= 회문, palindrome)

Our Stanley is Stanley Yelnats IV. His father is III, grandfather II, great-grandfather I.

이 책의 Stanley는 4세. 아버지가 3세, 할아버지가 2세, 증조할아버지가 1세.

What is a flashback? (13.11.2025 자료)

'The story of Holes takes place in a number of different time periods: the present, Stanley's recent past and 100 years ago. We learn that the past has a huge impact on the present, even if the characters don't realise it. Sometimes the stories of the past are told whilst the narrator continues to tell the story. You will see this in Chapter 6. This technique is called a flashback.'

'Holes 이야기는 여러 시간대에서 일어난다: 현재, Stanley의 최근 과거, 그리고 100년 전. 과거가 현재에 큰 영향을 미친다는 것을 배운다. 때로 과거 이야기가 화자가 계속 이야기하는 동안 들어간다. Chapter 6에서 볼 수 있다. 이 기법을 flashback(회상)이라 한다.'

Read this from Holes: '"Sweet Feet?" said X-Ray. "Yeah, right!"' and '"He'd led the American League in stolen bases over the last three years."' (From 14.11.2025 lesson) What do the words "sweet feet" infer about Clyde Livingston? Use evidence from the text.

✅ Model answer

The nickname 'Sweet Feet' infers that Clyde Livingston is very fast and skilful with his feet — a great runner.

'Sweet Feet' 별명은 Clyde Livingston이 발이 매우 빠르고 능숙하다는 것을 추론하게 합니다 — 훌륭한 주자.

The evidence is that he 'led the American League in stolen bases over the last three years' — stealing bases in baseball requires being a very fast runner.

근거: 그가 '지난 3년 동안 American League의 도루(stolen bases) 1위였다' — 야구에서 도루는 매우 빠른 주자에게 필요한 기술.

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Sensible inference (fast / skilful runner / great at stealing bases)
  • Uses evidence: stolen bases / led the league
  • Links the evidence to the inference
💡 Sweet Feet = 발이 빠르다 + 근거(stolen bases) + 연결.
Question 6 of 8 4 marks
Define the term FLASHBACK and explain why an author like Louis Sachar might use this technique in Holes. (From 13.11.2025 lesson objectives)

✅ Model answer

A flashback is a narrative technique in which the narrator tells stories from the past while the main story continues in the present.

Flashback은 주된 이야기가 현재에서 진행되는 동안 화자가 과거의 이야기를 들려주는 서술 기법입니다.

Louis Sachar uses flashbacks in Holes to show how the past has a huge impact on the present, even when the characters don't realise it.

Louis Sachar는 Holes에서 flashback을 사용해 과거가 현재에 큰 영향을 미친다는 것을 보여줍니다 — 등장인물들이 그것을 깨닫지 못해도.

For example, the story of Stanley's no-good-dirty-rotten-pig-stealing-great-great-grandfather's curse helps explain why Stanley keeps having bad luck.

예: Stanley의 'no-good-dirty-rotten-pig-stealing-great-great-grandfather'의 저주 이야기는 Stanley가 계속 불운한 이유를 설명합니다.

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Defines flashback (past scene during present-day story)
  • Mentions narrator / technique
  • Explains why authors use it (past affects present)
  • Uses an example from Holes
💡 정의 + 작가가 쓰는 이유 + Holes에서 예시 (저주, 운동화 이야기 등).
Question 7 of 8 6 marks
Decide whether each of these is SUSTAINABLE or NOT SUSTAINABLE tourism and explain why. Use linking words (because, so, therefore, as a result). (From Sustainable Tourism Starter)
(1) Building big resorts on beaches
(2) Using local guides and homestays
(3) Littering in national parks

✅ Model answer

(1) Building big resorts on beaches is NOT sustainable, because it destroys natural habitats and pollutes the sea. As a result, wildlife and beaches are damaged for the future.

(1) 해변에 큰 리조트 짓기 = 지속 불가. 자연 서식지를 파괴하고 바다를 오염시키므로, 결과적으로 야생동물과 해변이 미래에 손상됩니다.

(2) Using local guides and homestays IS sustainable, because it helps the local economy and respects local culture. Therefore, the benefits stay in the community.

(2) 현지 가이드·홈스테이 = 지속 가능. 지역 경제를 돕고 문화를 존중하므로, 따라서 이익이 공동체에 머뭅니다.

(3) Littering in national parks is NOT sustainable, so it harms wildlife and pollutes nature. Therefore, it destroys the environment future visitors should enjoy.

(3) 국립공원에 쓰레기 버리기 = 지속 불가. 야생동물을 해치고 자연을 오염시키므로, 미래 방문객이 누려야 할 환경을 파괴합니다.

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • (1) Correct classification + reason for resorts
  • (2) Correct classification + reason for local guides
  • (3) Correct classification + reason for littering
  • Uses at least 3 linking words (because, so, therefore, as a result)
💡 3개 모두 답 + 이유 + 연결어 (because/so/therefore/as a result).
Question 8 of 8 3 marks
Read this from Holes Chapter 1: 'The campers are forbidden to lie in the hammock. It belongs to the Warden. The Warden owns the shade.' What can you INFER about the Warden? Use evidence from the text. (CA1b-style)

✅ Model answer

I can infer that the Warden is very powerful and strict — she has control even over basic things like shade and rest.

Warden은 매우 강하고 엄격한 사람이라고 추론할 수 있습니다 — 그늘과 휴식 같은 기본적인 것까지 통제합니다.

The evidence is that the campers are 'forbidden' from lying in the hammock and that 'The Warden owns the shade' — these show she has total authority over the campers' comfort.

근거: campers는 hammock에 눕는 것이 '금지'되고 'Warden이 그늘을 소유한다' — campers의 편안함을 완전히 지배합니다.

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Sensible inference (powerful, strict, in charge)
  • Uses evidence ('forbidden' / 'Warden owns the shade')
  • Links evidence to inference
💡 추론 (강하고 엄격) + 근거 인용 + 연결.
