1. What is Speed?

Speed = distance ÷ time
📄 PDF

Speed definition (속도의 정의)

Cognito

Speed is how much distance an object travels per unit of time.

속도(speed)는 물체가 단위 시간에 이동하는 거리입니다.

Formula: Speed = Distance ÷ Time.

공식: 속도 = 거리 ÷ 시간.

Units of speed (속도의 단위)

Common units: metres per second (m/s), kilometres per hour (km/h), miles per hour (mph).

흔히 쓰는 단위: 초속(m/s), 시속(km/h), mph.

To convert km/h to m/s, divide by 3.6.

km/h를 m/s로 바꾸려면 3.6으로 나눕니다.

Average vs instantaneous speed (평균 vs 순간 속도)

Average speed is total distance ÷ total time over a journey.

평균 속도는 전체 이동 동안의 총 거리 ÷ 총 시간입니다.

Instantaneous speed is the speed at a single moment (e.g. what a car's speedometer shows right now).

순간 속도는 한 순간의 속도입니다 (예: 자동차 속도계가 지금 보여주는 값).

Example calculation (계산 예시)

A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed?

자동차가 2시간 동안 120km를 갑니다. 평균 속도는?

Speed = Distance ÷ Time = 120 ÷ 2 = 60 km/h.

속도 = 거리 ÷ 시간 = 120 ÷ 2 = 60 km/h.

2. Distance-Time Graphs

Reading motion graphs
📄 PDF

What is a distance-time graph? (거리-시간 그래프)

Cognito

A distance-time graph shows how the distance travelled by an object changes over time.

거리-시간 그래프는 물체가 이동한 거리가 시간에 따라 어떻게 변하는지 보여줍니다.

x-axis (horizontal): time. y-axis (vertical): distance.

x축(가로): 시간. y축(세로): 거리.

Reading the graph (그래프 읽는 법)

A flat (horizontal) line means the object is stationary (not moving).

평평한 선은 물체가 정지해 있음을 의미합니다.

A straight sloped line means the object is moving at constant speed.

직선으로 비스듬한 선은 일정한 속도로 움직이고 있음을 의미합니다.

A steeper slope means a faster speed.

경사가 가파를수록 속도가 빠릅니다.

A curved line means the speed is changing (accelerating or decelerating).

곡선은 속도가 변하고 있음을 의미합니다 (가속 또는 감속).

Calculating speed from a graph (그래프로 속도 계산)

The speed = gradient (slope) of the line.

속도 = 그래프 기울기입니다.

Speed = change in distance ÷ change in time over a section of the graph.

속도 = 거리 변화량 ÷ 시간 변화량 (그래프의 한 구간).

Example: if distance goes from 0 to 100 m in 20 s, speed = 100 ÷ 20 = 5 m/s.

예: 20초 동안 거리가 0→100m로 가면, 속도 = 100 ÷ 20 = 5 m/s.

Question 1 of 5 2 marks
Write the equation for SPEED and state two common units.

✅ Model answer

Equation: Speed = Distance ÷ Time.

공식: 속도 = 거리 ÷ 시간.

Common units: metres per second (m/s) and kilometres per hour (km/h).

흔한 단위: m/s와 km/h.

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Speed = distance ÷ time
  • Two valid units
💡 공식 + 단위 2개.
Question 2 of 5 2 marks
A runner travels 400 metres in 50 seconds. Calculate their average speed.

✅ Model answer

Speed = Distance ÷ Time = 400 ÷ 50 = 8 m/s.

속도 = 거리 ÷ 시간 = 400 ÷ 50 = 8 m/s.

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Correct equation used
  • Correct answer: 8 m/s (with unit)
💡 단위까지 적기 (8 m/s).
Question 3 of 5 2 marks
Explain the difference between AVERAGE speed and INSTANTANEOUS speed.

✅ Model answer

Average speed is the total distance travelled divided by the total time taken over a whole journey.

평균 속도는 여행 전체에서의 총 거리 ÷ 총 시간입니다.

Instantaneous speed is the speed at a single moment in time, e.g. what a car's speedometer shows right now.

순간 속도는 어느 한 순간의 속도입니다 (예: 자동차 속도계가 지금 보여주는 값).

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Average = total distance / total time
  • Instantaneous = speed at one moment
💡 평균(전체) vs 순간(한 순간).
Question 4 of 5 3 marks
Describe what each of these features on a DISTANCE-TIME GRAPH tells you about the motion: (a) a flat (horizontal) line, (b) a steep straight line, (c) a curved line.

✅ Model answer

(a) A flat line means the object is stationary (not moving) — distance is not changing.

(a) 평평한 선: 물체가 정지해 있음 — 거리가 변하지 않음.

(b) A steep straight line means the object is moving at a constant, fast speed.

(b) 가파른 직선: 일정하고 빠른 속도로 움직임.

(c) A curved line means the speed is changing (the object is accelerating or decelerating).

(c) 곡선: 속도가 변하고 있음 (가속 또는 감속).

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Flat line = stationary
  • Steep straight line = constant fast speed
  • Curved line = changing speed
💡 수평선=정지, 직선=등속, 곡선=속도 변함.
Question 5 of 5 2 marks
On a distance-time graph, the line goes from (0 s, 0 m) to (20 s, 100 m) in a straight line. Calculate the speed shown by this line.

✅ Model answer

Speed = gradient = change in distance ÷ change in time = (100 − 0) ÷ (20 − 0) = 100 ÷ 20 = 5 m/s.

속도 = 기울기 = 거리 변화 ÷ 시간 변화 = (100−0) ÷ (20−0) = 5 m/s.

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Uses gradient correctly
  • Correct answer: 5 m/s
💡 기울기 = 거리÷시간 = 5 m/s.
