1. Reading — All 3 Parts

Short-answer · Matching · Note-taking

Part 1: Short answer (10 questions)

Text type: informative article (~200 words) on an everyday theme (travel, food, school, sport…).

지문: 정보 기사 약 200단어 (여행·음식·학교·스포츠 등 일상 주제).

Strategy:

전략:

• Skim the text once to get the general idea.

• 먼저 훑어 읽고 전체 요지 파악.

• Read each question carefully — underline key words.

• 각 문제를 자세히 읽고 핵심어에 밑줄.

• Scan the text for the answer. Quote or paraphrase briefly.

• 답을 찾아 읽기. 짧게 인용 또는 바꿔 쓰기.

• Answers usually need just 1-5 words, not full sentences.

• 답은 보통 1-5단어로 충분, 완전한 문장 X.

Part 2: Matching (8 questions)

Text type: multiple short comments from different writers (vox-pop, internet forum, social media). About 200 words total.

지문: 여러 사람의 짧은 의견 (vox-pop, 포럼, 댓글). 총 약 200단어.

Task: match each commenter to their main idea (e.g. who thinks X? who agrees with Y?).

과제: 각 댓글 작성자를 그들의 주요 의견과 매칭.

Strategy:

전략:

• Read all the comments first.

• 모든 댓글을 먼저 읽기.

• Look for opinion words: 'I think', 'I love', 'I hate', 'in my view'.

• 의견 표현을 찾기: 'I think', 'I love', 'in my view' 등.

• Watch for synonyms — the question may rephrase the comment.

유의어에 주의 — 문제는 댓글을 다르게 표현할 수 있음.

Part 3: Note-taking (7 questions)

Text type: semi-formal essay with for/against, cause/solution, or advantages/disadvantages structure (~300 words).

지문: 찬반·원인-해결·장단점 구조의 준격식 에세이 약 300단어.

Task: fill in notes based on the text (usually a structured template).

과제: 텍스트 기반으로 노트 빈칸 채우기 (보통 표 형식).

Strategy:

전략:

• Recognise signposting: 'firstly', 'on the other hand', 'however', 'in conclusion'.

신호어 인식: 'firstly', 'on the other hand', 'however', 'in conclusion'.

• Each paragraph usually = one main point.

보통 한 문단 = 한 가지 요점.

• Notes should be brief — keywords or short phrases, not full sentences.

노트는 짧게 — 핵심어/짧은 구절, 완전한 문장 X.

2. Writing — Informal Email

60-80 단어, 10점

What is an informal email? (비격식 이메일이란?)

An informal email is written to someone you know well — a friend, family member, or classmate.

비격식 이메일은 잘 아는 사람(친구·가족·반친구)에게 쓰는 이메일입니다.

Tone: friendly, chatty, personal. Contractions OK (I'm, don't, can't).

분위기: 친근·구어체·개인적. 축약형 OK (I'm, don't, can't).

Length: 60-80 words. The prompt tells you the audience, purpose and points to include.

길이: 60-80 단어. 문제에 대상·목적·포함할 내용이 적혀 있습니다.

Structure (구조) — use this every time

1. Greeting: Hi/Hey [Name],

1. 인사: Hi/Hey [이름],

2. Opening: How are you? / Hope you're well. / Thanks for your email.

2. 시작: How are you? / Hope you're well. / Thanks for your email.

3. Main message: cover ALL the points in the prompt.

3. 본문: 문제의 모든 항목 다룸.

4. Closing: Can't wait to hear from you! / Write back soon!

4. 마무리: Can't wait to hear from you! / Write back soon!

5. Sign off: Love, / Take care, / See you soon, + your name.

5. 끝맺음: Love, / Take care, / See you soon, + 이름.

Useful phrases (자주 쓰는 표현)

Opening: How are you doing? / It's been ages! / Long time no see!

시작: How are you doing? / It's been ages! / Long time no see!

Sharing news: Guess what! / You won't believe what happened. / I'm so excited to tell you…

소식 공유: Guess what! / You won't believe what happened.

Inviting: Why don't you come over? / Fancy joining me? / Are you free on…?

초대: Why don't you come over? / Fancy joining me?

Asking: Let me know if you can come. / What do you think?

요청·질문: Let me know if you can come. / What do you think?

Model email (모범 이메일 예시)

Prompt: Email a friend about a film you watched recently. Say what it was, why you liked it, and recommend it.

문제: 최근 본 영화에 대해 친구에게 이메일. 무슨 영화인지, 왜 좋았는지, 추천하기.

Hi Mia,

Hope you're doing well! I just had to tell you about a film I watched last weekend — Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse. It's animated and absolutely stunning. The story is exciting and the music is amazing.

You'd love it because of the colours and the action scenes. Why don't we watch it together next time you're free?

Write back soon!
Love, Amy

(약 78 단어 — 인사 + 영화 정보 + 이유 + 추천 + 마무리 모두 포함)

3. Writing — Semi-formal Report

100-120 단어, 15점

What is a semi-formal report? (준격식 보고서)

A semi-formal report is written for someone in a school or community context — teacher, principal, club, student council.

준격식 보고서는 학교·동아리 맥락에서 (선생님·교장·학생회 등) 쓰는 글입니다.

Tone: polite, neutral, organised. NO contractions (write 'do not' instead of 'don't').

분위기: 예의 있고 중립적·정돈된. 축약형 금지 ('do not' O, 'don't' X).

Length: 100-120 words. Topic is usually Grade 6-related (school, environment, sport, culture).

길이: 100-120 단어. 주제는 보통 학교·환경·스포츠·문화.

Structure (구조)

1. Title / Heading: Report on [topic] — for [audience].

1. 제목: Report on [주제] — for [대상].

2. Introduction (1 sentence): state the purpose of the report.

2. 도입 (1문장): 보고서 목적 명시.

3. Main body (2-3 short paragraphs): cover the points in the prompt. Use sub-headings if helpful.

3. 본문 (2-3 짧은 문단): 문제 항목 다루기. 소제목 OK.

4. Conclusion / Recommendation: one suggestion or summary sentence.

4. 결론·제안: 제안 또는 요약 한 문장.

Linking words for reports (보고서용 연결어)

Adding: in addition, furthermore, moreover.

추가: in addition, furthermore, moreover.

Contrasting: however, on the other hand, nevertheless.

대조: however, on the other hand, nevertheless.

Cause/effect: therefore, consequently, as a result.

원인·결과: therefore, consequently, as a result.

Examples: for example, for instance, such as.

예시: for example, for instance, such as.

Concluding: in conclusion, to sum up, overall.

결론: in conclusion, to sum up, overall.

Model report (모범 보고서 예시)

Prompt: Write a report for the head teacher about whether the school should introduce a recycling programme.

문제: 학교가 재활용 프로그램을 도입해야 할지 교장 선생님께 보고서.

Report on Introducing a Recycling Programme

This report outlines the benefits of starting a recycling programme at our school.

Firstly, recycling would significantly reduce the amount of waste sent to landfill. In addition, it would teach students valuable lessons about caring for the environment.

However, some challenges exist. For example, separate bins would need to be purchased, and pupils would need clear instructions.

In conclusion, the advantages outweigh the disadvantages. I strongly recommend that the school introduces a simple, three-bin recycling system in every classroom.

(약 105 단어 — 제목 + 도입 + 장점 + 단점 + 결론·제안 + 연결어 다양)

4. High-scoring Vocabulary

Holiday adjectives + academic words

Why high-level vocabulary matters (왜 고급 어휘가 중요?)

Examiners give higher marks when you use a range of appropriate vocabulary.

채점관은 다양하고 적절한 어휘를 쓸 때 더 높은 점수를 줍니다.

Don't just write 'nice' or 'good' — try specific, vivid words.

'nice'나 'good'만 쓰지 말고 구체적이고 생생한 단어를 시도하세요.

❌ Basic: The hotel was nice.

❌ 기초: The hotel was nice.

✅ Better: The hotel was idyllic, with picturesque gardens.

✅ 더 좋음: The hotel was idyllic, with picturesque gardens.

Positive holiday adjectives (긍정 여행 형용사)

picturesque – very pretty, like a painting. vibrant – full of life and energy. serene – calm and peaceful. majestic – grand, impressive. bustling – full of busy activity. idyllic – perfect, dream-like. enchanting – magical, charming. unspoilt – natural, untouched.

picturesque(그림같이 예쁜), vibrant(활기찬), serene(고요한), majestic(웅장한), bustling(북적이는), idyllic(목가적·꿈같이 완벽한), enchanting(매혹적인), unspoilt(때 묻지 않은).

Climate & landscape (기후·풍경)

Climate: tropical (덥고 습한), refreshing (시원한), mild (온화한), sweltering (찌는 듯한), breezy (바람 부는).

기후: tropical, refreshing, mild, sweltering, breezy.

Landscape: breathtaking (숨막힐 듯 아름다운), lush (푸르른), towering (우뚝 솟은), rolling (완만한), rugged (거친·자연 그대로).

풍경: breathtaking, lush, towering, rolling, rugged.

Cultural: historic (역사적인), ancient (고대의), quaint (예스럽고 매력적인), modern, cosmopolitan (다문화의).

문화·인공: historic, ancient, quaint, modern, cosmopolitan.

Academic words for any topic (모든 주제에 좋은 학술 어휘)

Replace boring words with these:

지루한 단어를 이것들로 바꿔보세요:

• important → significant, crucial, essential

• important → significant(중요한)/crucial(결정적인)/essential(필수적인)

• show → demonstrate, reveal, indicate

• show → demonstrate/reveal/indicate

• big → significant, substantial, considerable

• big → significant/substantial/considerable

• get → obtain, receive, acquire

• get → obtain/receive/acquire

• think → believe, consider, suggest

• think → believe/consider/suggest

5. Grammar & Punctuation Essentials

시제·문장구조·구두점 핵심

Pick a tense and stick to it (시제 일관성)

Don't switch between past and present without reason. Examiners notice tense errors quickly.

이유 없이 과거와 현재를 왔다 갔다 하지 마세요. 채점관이 바로 알아챕니다.

❌ Wrong: Yesterday I go to the beach and saw dolphins.

❌ 틀림: 'go'와 'saw'가 시제 불일치.

✅ Right: Yesterday I went to the beach and saw dolphins.

✅ 맞음: Yesterday I went to the beach and saw dolphins.

Tip: for emails about past holidays use past simple. For reports on current issues use present.

팁: 지난 여행 이메일은 과거 단순, 현재 이슈 보고서는 현재.

Vary your sentence types (문장 다양하게)

Top-band writing uses both simple and complex sentences.

상위 점수는 단순문과 복문을 모두 씁니다.

Simple: I love reading.

단순문: I love reading.

Compound (and/but/so): I love reading, but my brother prefers gaming.

중문 (and/but/so): I love reading, but my brother prefers gaming.

Complex (because/although/when): Although I am tired, I will finish my homework.

복문 (because/although/when): Although I am tired, I will finish my homework.

Top connectives to use (꼭 쓸 연결어 TOP)

Adding: in addition, moreover, furthermore

추가: in addition, moreover, furthermore

Contrast: however, although, on the other hand

대조: however, although, on the other hand

Cause/effect: therefore, consequently, as a result

원인·결과: therefore, consequently, as a result

Examples: for example, for instance, such as

예시: for example, for instance, such as

Order: firstly, next, finally, in conclusion

순서: firstly, next, finally, in conclusion

Punctuation quick check (구두점 빠른 확인)

Apostrophes: don't = do not (contraction), Tom's bag = the bag belonging to Tom (possession).

어퍼스트로피: don't(축약), Tom's bag(소유).

its vs it's: 'its' = belonging to it (the dog wagged its tail). 'it's' = it is (it's raining).

its vs it's: its=소유, it's=it is.

Speech marks: "I'm going home," she said. (comma/full stop inside the quotes)

큰따옴표: 쉼표·마침표는 따옴표 안쪽.

Capital letters: at the start of every sentence + names + days/months + 'I'.

대문자: 문장 시작·이름·요일/월·'I'.

📝 Written-answer practice · 시험 형식: 서술형

Question 1 of 7 3 marks
You have 60 minutes in total for the EAL EoY exam. Reading has 25 questions across 3 texts, and Writing has 2 tasks. Suggest a sensible time-plan.

✅ Model answer

Reading: about 30 minutes — roughly 10 minutes per text (skim, then answer questions).

Reading: 약 30분 — 지문당 10분 (훑어 읽고, 문제 풀기).

Writing: about 25 minutes — 10 minutes for the informal email, 15 minutes for the longer semi-formal report.

Writing: 약 25분 — 비격식 이메일 10분, 준격식 보고서 15분.

Check: 5 minutes at the end for spelling, punctuation and missing words.

검토: 5분 — 철자·구두점·빠진 단어 확인.

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Reasonable Reading time (~30 min)
  • Reasonable Writing time (~25 min, longer for report)
  • Includes checking time at end
💡 읽기 30분 + 쓰기 25분 + 검토 5분.
Question 2 of 7 10 marks
Write an INFORMAL EMAIL (60-80 words) to a friend about a film or book you enjoyed recently. Say what it was, why you liked it, and recommend it.

✅ Model answer

Hi Sam,

Hope you're doing well! I just had to tell you about a brilliant book I finished last week — Holes by Louis Sachar. It's an unusual story about a boy sent to a strange desert camp, full of mystery and twists.

You'd love it because of the surprises in every chapter. Why don't you borrow my copy next time you come over?

Write back soon!
Love, Amy

(약 76 단어 — 인사, 영화·책 정보, 좋은 이유, 추천, 마무리)

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Friendly greeting (Hi/Hey + name)
  • Friendly opening (Hope you're well / How are you)
  • All 3 content points: what it was, why liked, recommendation
  • Closing line + sign-off (Love/See you/Write soon + name)
  • Informal language + contractions (I'm, you'd, don't)
  • 60-80 words (not too short, not too long)
💡 인사 + 정보 + 이유 + 추천 + 끝맺음 5가지 다 포함, 친근한 톤.
Question 3 of 7 15 marks
Write a SEMI-FORMAL REPORT (100-120 words) for your head teacher recommending one change to the school cafeteria. Include: current problem, suggested change, expected benefit.

✅ Model answer

Report on Improving the School Cafeteria

This report recommends introducing a wider variety of healthy meal options in the school cafeteria.

At present, the menu is repetitive and contains few vegetarian or low-sugar choices. Many students bring food from home as a result. Furthermore, the long queues at lunchtime mean some pupils do not have time to eat properly.

I therefore suggest adding two healthy daily specials and a salad bar. In addition, opening a second serving counter would significantly reduce queueing time.

In conclusion, these changes would encourage healthier eating and improve the lunch experience for the whole school.

(약 112 단어 — 제목 + 도입 + 현재 문제 + 제안 + 결론, 연결어 다양)

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Clear title / heading
  • Introductory sentence stating the purpose
  • Covers all 3 content points (problem, change, benefit)
  • Semi-formal tone (no contractions)
  • Variety of linking words (Furthermore, In addition, Therefore, In conclusion)
  • Conclusion / recommendation paragraph
  • 100-120 words
💡 제목 + 도입 + 문제·제안·이익 + 결론, 연결어 다양 사용, 축약형 X.

Question 7 of 7 3 marks
Read this comment from a vox-pop about school uniforms: "Honestly, I dread getting dressed every morning. I just want to wear something that feels like me." What can you INFER about this writer's opinion on school uniforms, and what is your evidence?

✅ Model answer

I can infer the writer dislikes school uniforms and would prefer to wear their own clothes.

이 사람은 교복을 싫어하고 자기 옷을 입고 싶어 한다고 추론할 수 있습니다.

The evidence is the word 'dread' (a strong negative feeling about getting dressed in uniform) and the phrase 'something that feels like me', which suggests the uniform does not let them express their personality.

근거: 'dread'(교복 입는 것을 두려워함), 'something that feels like me'(교복은 자신을 표현하게 해주지 않음을 시사).

🎯 Marking points (look for these)
  • Sensible inference (dislikes uniform / prefers own clothes)
  • Quotes/refers to evidence ('dread')
  • Quotes/refers to second piece of evidence ('feels like me') + links to inference
💡 추론 (싫어함) + 근거 2개 ('dread', 'feels like me') + 연결.
